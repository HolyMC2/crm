## 2026-11-25 — «Saltar con letras»: `f` puts a letter on everything you can press (lane teclado-letras; not deployed)

- **Why:** Marco: «i use vimium on my browser, f and all buttons highlight with a button combination, if i finish the combination it clicks, so useful to quickly learn shortcuts and movements … under a flag but i really want it».
- **Change:** a switch per person and per browser, off at first, at the foot of Alt+H («Saltar con letras (F)») and in Ctrl+K («Encender / Apagar saltar con letras»). With it on, `f` outside a text field puts a tag on every control on screen (only the open dialog's when there is one), and typing the tag presses it the way a mouse would (menus that open on pointerdown open), or puts the cursor in a field. Shift+F opens a link in another tab; Esc, a click, a scroll or a key with Ctrl/⌘ take the tags away; Backspace undoes a letter. Each tag also shows the control's own key, to learn them while jumping: `G → H` on the rail, `A` on a record's bolt, `.` on «⋯», `Ctrl+K`, `Ctrl+J`, `[`, `P` and `E` on a product, Ctrl+↵ on a dialog's button. While it is on, `f` is the letters' everywhere: Compras' «Registrar factura» is pressed through its tag, and Alt+H lists the letters instead of it. Tags never cover each other (a colliding one moves down). Taller, POS and the other apps framed in the shell keep their own keys (their frame is another document).
- **Proof:** live preview on doco-mirror, 1280 light and dark: off by default, on from Alt+H, 45 tags on MOD00526 with their keys, `sm` opened «Cambiar precio», a rail tag went to Hoy, inside the dialog only its 5 controls got tags. Tests: `letterHints.test.js` (new: tags, targets, keys shown, jumping, Shift+F, the switch, `f` over a page's own `f`, Alt+H rows), shell suites (707 passed). Rollback: revert; the switch is off for everyone until turned on.
